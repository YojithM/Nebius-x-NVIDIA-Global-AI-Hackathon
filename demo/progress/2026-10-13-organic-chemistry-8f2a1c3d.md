# Quiz — organic chemistry

Asked for by the student via page. Answers appear as they are given.

1. What is the rate-determining step of an SN1 reaction?
   - expected: loss of the leaving group to form a carbocation
   - answer: losing the chloride first
   - verdict: correct
2. Which intermediate is more stable, a primary or a tertiary carbocation?
   - expected: tertiary, because three alkyl groups donate electron density
   - answer: the primary one, it is less crowded
   - verdict: incorrect
3. What kind of solvent speeds up an SN1 reaction?
   - expected: a polar protic solvent, which stabilises the carbocation
   - answer: polar protic
   - verdict: correct
